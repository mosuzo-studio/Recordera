What it does

Record, capture and send — in one pass

Most tools make you record in one app, edit in a second and share from a third. Recordera collapses all three into a single hotkey.

Select and record

Drag once to set the frame and see its pixel size live. What lands in the file is exactly the region you chose — no cropping afterwards.

Capture and annotate

Draw arrows, add text, or blur anything private the moment you capture. You never need to open a separate image editor.

Send in one click

When you stop, the file is already on your clipboard. Paste it into a chat, a doc or a ticket and it is on its way.

Under the hood

Small things that add up

Recordera is deliberately narrow. It does a handful of things well instead of everything badly.

Precise framing

See the exact width and height as you drag, and snap to window edges when you want a clean cut.

A cursor that reads

Cursor movement is smoothed and clicks are enlarged, so viewers follow the point without hunting for it.

Written straight to disk

Recording is written locally at up to 4K 60fps with no compression pass and no upload in between.

Hotkey first

One press starts, one press stops. Recording never pulls you out of whatever you were doing.

Privacy

Your recordings stay yours

Recordera uploads nothing by default. Video is written on your machine, processed on your machine, and deleted when you say so. There is no account to create and no cloud to trust.

  • Recordings are saved locally, never queued for upload
  • Works with the network switched off entirely
  • No account, no telemetry on your content, no background traffic
  • Delete a file and it is gone — there is no second copy

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is Recordera free?
Yes. Recording is free, there is no watermark, and there is no time limit on a single recording.
Which version of Windows is supported?
Windows 10 and 11. Recordera ships as a single .exe installer — download it and run it.
Do my recordings go to a server?
No. Every recording and screenshot stays on your own machine. Recordera does not upload anything automatically, and it keeps working with the network off.
Can I record audio?
Yes. Your microphone and system audio can be captured together, and they stay in sync with the video.
Is there a recording time limit?
None. Record for as long as you need — the only limit is the space left on your disk.
